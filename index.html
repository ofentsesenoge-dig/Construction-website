About us

One team. Every stage of the project.

Gabosi OCC (Pty) Ltd provides professional services across the construction, built environment and mining sectors. We support clients from project planning and design through to construction delivery and project completion.

We are committed to delivering quality, cost-effective and practical solutions that respond to the specific requirements of each project, with a focus on professional service, sound project coordination, cost control, quality workmanship and adherence to industry standards, safety requirements and project timelines.
